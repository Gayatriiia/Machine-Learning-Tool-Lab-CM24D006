Key Findings
Precision Ceiling
Single-pose models achieve a 14.6 percentage-point mAP advantage in isolated-subject scenarios. The absence of inter-person NMS suppression eliminates a primary source of false negatives at close body-part proximity.
Multi-Pose Accuracy Degrades Non-Linearly
Adding each additional person reduces average keypoint confidence by ~3–4% due to expanded heatmap search space, occlusion ambiguity, and grouping errors in bottom-up architectures.
Occlusion Inversion
At >50% body occlusion, multi-pose models surprisingly outperform single-pose (+9 pp PCK) due to contextual reasoning across visible body parts of nearby individuals.
Distal Keypoint Gap
Wrist and ankle accuracy diverges most — single-pose leads by ~17 pp. Distal joints are the primary casualty of multi-person heatmap overlap since they are spatially closest across individual boundaries.
Latency × Accuracy Trade-off
Multi-pose inference costs 3–4× more compute per frame. For real-time applications (>30 fps), single-pose is the only viable option on edge hardware without GPU acceleration.
Practical Recommendation
Use single-pose for fitness/rehab apps, AR filters, or sports analytics with one subject. Switch to multi-pose for surveillance, crowd analytics, dance/choreography, and team sports.
